About Fun Math Club Activities

Fun Math Club activities are interactive web pages of math puzzles, problems, and games for math clubs and math circles. The target audience is grades 7-12, but younger and older students may enjoy the activities as well. Each activity has a "classroom" providing some background on the activity and how to use the remaining pages. Most activities have a "laboratory" where students can experience the interactive activity and experiment with variations. There may also be additional pages with additional problems, extensions, and related activities.

As of the time of creating these pages, I have no plans to include problem sets and solutions. Most activities are well known and it is expected that if these pages are used in a math club or math circle, the session leader will have the background to turn the activity into a lesson.
